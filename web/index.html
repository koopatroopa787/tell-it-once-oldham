What happens to your answers

First
Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses are taken out of whatever you type.
Then
The AI picks from the sixteen Oldham places we've checked, and says why each one might suit you. Anything it makes up gets thrown away.
If you're not okay
Some things, like having nowhere to sleep tonight, bring up who to call straight away. That part never waits on the AI.
After
Nothing is saved. If you use the mic, your voice goes through ElevenLabs to turn it into text.
